04 — Projects

Engineering Projects

🌡️
Thermal · CFD
Cooling Efficiency Analysis of an Automobile Radiator

Used the ANSYS Fluent Flow module to calculate and compare the cooling efficiency of a car radiator across three different materials — gold, aluminum, and titanium — with a single working fluid. Thermal distribution and fluid flow behavior were analyzed to determine the optimal material for maximum heat dissipation performance.

ANSYS Fluent CFD Aluminum Titanium Gold
⚙️
Structural · FEA
Structural Analysis of a Slat Chain Conveyor System

Performed load-bearing analysis of a slat chain conveyor system installed on a 7-meter track, driven by a 2.2 kW motor, designed to carry 6 boxes each weighing 50 kg. The static structural module in ANSYS was used to evaluate stress distribution, deformation, and safety factors under full operational load.

ANSYS Static Structural FEA Stress Analysis 2.2 kW Motor 7m Track
🎯
Graduation Project · Defense
Ballistic Impact Analysis on Ti-6Al-4V

Investigated ballistic effects and deformation characteristics of NATO standard 7.62 mm, 5.56 mm, and 9 mm ammunition on Ti-6Al-4V titanium alloy — a critical aerospace and defense material. Simulations executed at high strain rates via integrated solvers.

ANSYS Explicit Dynamics LS-DYNA AUTODYN Ti-6Al-4V NATO 7.62mm
